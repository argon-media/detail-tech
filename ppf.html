What is PPF

So, what is paint protection film?

Paint protection film, or PPF, is a clear, tough urethane layer applied right over your vehicle's paint. Think of it as an invisible shield. It takes the rock chips, scratches, salt, and road debris that would otherwise hit your finish, and it does it without changing how your car looks. The top layer even self-heals light scratches with a little heat, from the sun or warm water. Once it's on, your factory paint stays perfect underneath.

  • Blocks rock chips, scratches, and road debris
  • Self-healing top layer that clears light swirls with heat
  • Keeps dirt from bonding, so washing stays easy
  • Preserves your paint and your resale value

How it works

Clean. Cut. Install. Inspect.

1

Prep & Decontaminate

We hand wash, clay bar, and decontaminate the paint so the surface is spotless before any film touches it.

2

Measure & Cut

Film is precision-cut to your exact panels, so it wraps edges clean with no guesswork.

3

Install

We lay the film and work out every bubble by hand, tucking edges so the film disappears into the panel.

4

Cure & Inspect

The film sets, then we re-check every edge to confirm it's sealed and flawless before you pick up.
