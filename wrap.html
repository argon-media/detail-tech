What is a wrap

So, what is a vinyl wrap?

A vinyl wrap is a colored or printed film applied over your paint. It can completely change your color, add custom accents, or black out the chrome, all without touching the factory paint underneath. On healthy paint, a proper wrap protects what's beneath it and comes off clean when you want a change. It's the fastest way to transform how your vehicle looks.

  • Full color changes in over 200 colors and finishes
  • Gloss, satin, matte, and specialty films
  • Custom accents, stripes, roof, mirrors, and hood wraps
  • Protects your factory paint underneath
Colors and finishes from 3M and Avery Dennison.
