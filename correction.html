What is paint correction

So, what is paint correction?

Over time, washing, drying, and everyday driving leave your paint covered in fine swirl marks, scratches, and haze that dull the finish. Paint correction is the process of carefully machine polishing the clear coat to remove those imperfections instead of hiding them. The result is paint that looks deep, glossy, and clear again, a level of refinement waxing or simple polishing can't match.

  • Removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation
  • Restores a deep, glossy, reflective finish
  • Measured and done to protect your clear coat
  • The essential first step before a ceramic coating

How it works

Measured. Corrected. Refined.

We don't just start cutting. We read the paint first, then correct only as much as needed to protect your clear coat.

1

Wash & Decontaminate

A full hand wash, iron decontamination, and clay bar so the surface is perfectly clean before we touch it with a machine.

2

Measure the Paint

We use a paint depth reader to see how much clear coat there is to work with, so correction is safe.

3

Cut

A cutting pad and compound remove the swirls, scratches, and defects from the clear coat.

4

Refine

A softer pad and polish follow to bring back the deep, mirror gloss across the whole car.
