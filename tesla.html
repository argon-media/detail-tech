Tesla Services

Three ways to protect it.

Film, gloss, and tint. Most Tesla owners end up combining all three. Here's exactly what each one does and what's included.

Film

Paint Protection Film

Teslas come from the factory with thin, soft paint that can start chipping the moment you hit the highway. Our paint protection film adds a clear, self-healing layer over the panels that take the most hits. That way rock chips, sand, and road salt hit the film instead of your paint. Go with a partial front for the key spots, or full-body coverage to protect the whole car. Available in shiny gloss or stealth matte.

  • Clear, self-healing film
  • Partial front to full-body
  • Gloss or stealth matte
  • Up to a 10-year warranty

Gloss

Ceramic Coating

A System X ceramic coating bonds to the paint and locks in a deep, glossy finish that beads water and shrugs off dirt. Your Tesla stays cleaner between washes, resists swirls and water spots, and keeps that just-detailed look for years. Every coating starts with a full paint correction, so we seal in flawless paint instead of the swirls it came with.

  • System X ceramic protection
  • Paint correction included
  • Hydrophobic, easy-clean finish
  • Years of gloss and UV defense

Tint

Window Tint

Ceramic window tint does far more than darken the glass. It rejects a large share of the sun's heat so your cabin stays cooler and the battery works less to keep up, blocks over 99% of the UV rays that fade and crack your interior, and cuts glare on long drives. Dialed-in shades for every window, backed by a lifetime warranty.

  • Heat-rejecting ceramic tint
  • Blocks 99%+ of UV rays
  • Less glare, cooler cabin
  • Backed by a lifetime warranty

The smart setup

PPF up front. Ceramic over the top.

The setup most Tesla owners land on is paint protection film on the high-impact areas, then a ceramic coating over the whole car. The film takes the physical hits, and the ceramic adds gloss and makes washing easy. Tint the windows to cut the heat, and the car is dialed in. We'll help you build the right combination for how you drive and how long you're keeping it.
