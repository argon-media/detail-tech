What is PDR

So, what is paintless dent repair?

Paintless dent repair, or PDR, is a technique for removing dents without touching your paint. Using specialized tools, we gently massage the metal back to its original shape from behind the panel. Because there's no filler, no sanding, and no repainting, your factory finish stays intact, which keeps the repair invisible and your vehicle's value where it should be.

  • No fillers, no sanding, no repainting
  • Keeps your original factory paint
  • Faster and cleaner than traditional body work
  • Preserves your resale value

Good to know

Is your dent a candidate?

PDR is ideal when the paint is not cracked, chipped, or broken and the metal hasn't been stretched too far. It works on most door dings, minor dents, and light creases. Sharp dents on an edge, or dents where the paint is already damaged, may need a different repair, and we'll tell you straight up after we see it.

  • Paint is still intact, not cracked or chipped
  • The dent isn't on a sharp panel edge
  • The metal hasn't been badly stretched
  • Send us a photo for a quick assessment
