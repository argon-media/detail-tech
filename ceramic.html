What is ceramic coating

So, what is a ceramic coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds to your paint and cures into a hard, clear layer. It is not a wax that washes off in a month. It is a long-term shield that beads water, resists dirt and brake dust, blocks UV fade, and holds a deep gloss for years. The result is easier washing and paint that stays sharp far longer.

  • Water and dirt bead off instead of sticking
  • Deep, durable gloss that lasts for years
  • Protects against UV, brake dust, and contaminants
  • Makes every future wash faster and easier

Revivify

A coating that heals itself.

Revivify is an advanced, silicon-based self-healing coating that behaves more like paint protection film than traditional ceramic. Minor scratches and swirl marks actually heal, something no standard ceramic can do. It is warrantied for up to 5 years and helps boost your CarFax value.
